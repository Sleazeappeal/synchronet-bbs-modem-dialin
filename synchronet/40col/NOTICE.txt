Synchronet 40-column (C64/PETSCII) package
==========================================

These files adapt Synchronet BBS (https://www.synchro.net),
Copyright Rob Swindell, for 40-column terminals. Synchronet is
free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or
(at your option) any later version, and so are these files: see
LICENSE in the repository root and
https://www.synchro.net/copyright.html.

- mods/logonlist.js: modified copy of Synchronet exec/logonlist.js
  (one line per caller on narrow terminals)
- text/answer.40col.msg, mods/text/system.40col.msg and
  mods/text/menu/*.40col.*: 40-column versions of Synchronet's
  stock screens
- mods/logon.js, install.sh: new
